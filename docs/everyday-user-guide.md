# Learning to Use RetailPro
### A Simple Guide for Store Staff

A step-by-step guide to using the till — written for people who have never used a computer program like this before.

> ✓ No computer experience needed — just follow along.

---

## 1. Opening RetailPro

Every time you start your shift, this is how you get to the till screen.

1. **Turn on the computer or tablet.** Wait for it to fully switch on and show the normal desktop.
2. **Open the internet browser.** This is the app you use to look at websites — it might be called Chrome, Edge, or Firefox. Double-click its icon to open it.
3. **Go to the RetailPro address.** Your manager will have given you an address to type at the top of the browser. If it's already saved as a shortcut, just click that instead.

   > **Tip:** Ask your manager to save this page as a shortcut on the desktop for you, so you never have to type anything to get started.

4. **Wait for the Sign In screen to appear.** You'll see a dark screen with the RetailPro name and two boxes — one for your email, one for your password.

---

## 2. Signing In

Your manager will give you your own email and password. Keep these private — don't share them with other staff.

1. **Click inside the box that says "Email."** Type your email address exactly as your manager gave it to you.
2. **Click inside the box that says "Password."** Type your password. The letters will show as dots to keep it private — that's normal. There's a small eye icon (👁) you can click if you want to check what you typed.
3. **Click the green Sign In button.** If everything was typed correctly, you'll go straight to your main screen.

> **If it says "Invalid email or password":** Double-check you typed your email and password exactly right — capital letters matter. If it still doesn't work, ask your manager; only they can fix this for you.

---

## 3. Finding Your Way Around

Once you're signed in, here's what you're looking at.

**On the left side** of the screen is a list of everything you can do — this is called the menu. It might show things like:

- 🛒 Point of Sale
- 🧾 Sales History
- 📦 Products

You may not see every option — what you see depends on what your manager has allowed for your account. That's completely normal.

**At the top** of the screen, you'll always see the name of the page you're currently on, so you always know where you are.

To go anywhere else, just click a different name in the left-side menu — you never need to use the browser's back button.

---

## 4. Making a Sale

This is what you'll do the most, so take your time going through it once and it will quickly become second nature.

1. **Click "Point of Sale" in the left menu.** This opens the till screen where you ring up customer purchases.

2. **Add the customer's items.** There are two ways to do this:
   - **Scanning:** Hold the item's barcode up to the scanner and wait for the beep. The item appears automatically — you don't need to click anything first.
   - **No scanner, or item won't scan:** Type the product's name into the search box, or tap its picture in the grid on the screen.

3. **Check the list on the right side.** Every item you've added shows up on the receipt-shaped list on the right, along with its price. This updates every time you add something.

4. **If the customer wants more than one of something,** find that item in the list and tap the small **+** button to add another one, or **−** to take one away.

5. **If you added something by mistake,** find it in the list and tap the word **remove** next to it. It disappears from the sale completely.

6. **Ask the customer how they'd like to pay.** See [Getting Paid](#6-getting-paid) below for exactly what to do for each payment type.

7. **Tap the big green Charge button at the bottom** (it will show the total, e.g. `Charge $12.40`). This finishes the sale.

8. **Hand over the receipt and any change.** A receipt will print automatically if a receipt printer is connected. Give it to the customer along with any change owed.

9. **The screen clears itself.** You're now ready to start the next customer's sale — there's nothing extra you need to click.

> **Made a mistake after tapping Charge?** Once a sale is completed, you can't undo it yourself. Let your manager know — they can process a refund or void the sale from Sales History. Don't try to "fix" it by ringing up a second sale.

---

## 5. Selling by the Box, or Selling Loose

Some products — like a case of drinks or a box of medicine — can be sold either as a
full box or as single loose units, and the till handles both from the same product.

1. **Selling one loose unit (e.g. one bottle):** scan the bottle's own barcode as
   normal, or tap the product's picture in the grid. This works exactly like any
   other item.

2. **Selling a full box:** if a product can be sold by the box, you'll see a second,
   smaller button under its picture in the grid, like **"+ Box of 12 — $9.99."** Tap
   that instead to add one full box to the sale at the box price. You can also scan
   the barcode printed on the box itself — the till recognizes both barcodes
   automatically and knows which one you scanned.

3. **Mixing both in one sale is fine.** A customer can buy 1 box and 2 loose bottles
   in the same transaction — just add each the way described above. They'll show as
   separate lines on the receipt, one saying "(Box of 12)" and one without.

> **Not every product can be sold by the box.** If you only see the picture with no
> second button underneath, that item hasn't been set up for box selling — ask your
> manager if you think it should be.

## 6. Getting Paid

Before tapping Charge, tap the button that matches how the customer is paying: **Cash · Card · Mobile Money · Bank Transfer · Split Pay · On Credit**

### 💵 If the customer pays Cash
Tap **Cash**, then type the exact amount of money the customer handed you into the box that appears. The screen will instantly tell you how much change to give back — hand that amount to the customer along with the receipt.

### 💳 If the customer pays by Card, Mobile Money, or Bank Transfer
Tap the matching button. Since the customer's payment machine or app handles the money itself, you don't need to type any amount — just complete their payment on that device as normal, then tap Charge here.

### ✂️ If the customer wants to pay part cash, part card
Tap **Split Pay**. Two boxes appear — type how much they're paying in **cash** in the
first one, and how much on **card/mobile money/bank transfer** in the second. A message
under the boxes tells you if the two amounts add up to the total yet — the Charge button
won't work until they do.

### 📝 If the customer wants to pay later ("On Credit")
This should only be used if your manager allows it for trusted regular customers. Tap **On Credit**, then find and select the customer's name from the list. If their name isn't there, ask a manager to add them first.

## 7. Reprinting a Receipt

If a customer loses their receipt or asks for another copy, you don't need to redo the sale.

1. **Click "Sales History" in the left menu.** This shows every sale that's been made, most recent at the top.
2. **Find the sale.** Look for the date and time it happened, or the amount, to spot the right one.
3. **Click "View" next to it.** This opens the full details of that sale.
4. **Click "🖨️ Print Receipt."** A new copy will print, exactly like the original.

---

## 8. If a Customer Wants to Return Something

Cashiers don't process refunds directly — this needs a Manager or Admin. Here's what
to do:

1. **Get a Manager or Admin.** Explain what the customer is returning and why.
2. **They'll open Sales History**, find the original sale, and process the refund or
   void it from there — the system automatically works out the correct refund amount
   and puts the returned stock back.
3. **You don't need to do anything else** — once they've processed it, the sale's
   status updates automatically to show it was refunded.

> If you're a Manager or Admin yourself, see the separate **Admin Team Setup Guide**
> for exactly how to process a refund or void a sale.

---

## 9. Checking a Price or Stock Level

If a customer asks whether something is in stock, or you need to double-check a price without starting a sale.

1. **Click "Products" or "Inventory" in the left menu.** Both show every item the store sells — Inventory focuses on how much stock is left.
2. **Type the item's name into the search box.** The list will narrow down as you type.
3. **Look at the colored label next to the item.** A green label means it's well stocked. An orange label saying **"Reorder Soon"** means stock is getting low — worth mentioning to your manager.

---

## 10. Signing Out

Always sign out when you finish your shift or step away from the till for a while — this keeps the store's sales and money information private and safe.

1. **Look at the bottom of the left-side menu.** You'll see your name and a button underneath it.
2. **Click "Sign out."** You'll be taken back to the Sign In screen, ready for the next person to log in with their own account.

---

## 11. Common Problems & What To Do

**The barcode scanner isn't adding anything when I scan.**
Make sure the scanner's cable is plugged in firmly, and try clicking into the search box at the top of the Point of Sale screen first, then scan again. If it still doesn't work, add the item by typing its name instead, and let your manager know the scanner needs checking.

**I can't find an item when I search for it.**
Try typing just the first word of its name, in case of a small spelling difference. If it still doesn't appear, the item may not have been added to the system yet — ask your manager.

**I forgot my password.**
You can't reset this yourself. Ask your administrator or manager to set you a new one.

**The receipt didn't print.**
Check that the printer is switched on and has paper. You can always print it again — go to Sales History, find the sale, click View, then Print Receipt.

**The screen looks frozen or stuck.**
Click the refresh/reload button in your browser (usually a circular arrow near the top of the screen), or press the F5 key on the keyboard. You will need to sign in again, but nothing will be lost.

**I don't see an option my manager told me I should have.**
What you can see depends on the type of account your manager set up for you. Ask them to check your account's role and adjust it if needed.

## 12. Simple Word Guide

| Word | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Barcode** | The stripy black-and-white pattern printed on a product that the scanner reads to identify it. |
| **SKU** | A short code the store uses to identify a product — like a nickname for it. |
| **Till / POS** | The screen where you ring up a customer's purchase — short for "Point of Sale." |
| **Stock** | How many of an item the store currently has available to sell. |
| **Reorder Level** | The point at which stock is considered low and needs restocking soon. |
| **Supplier** | A business the store buys products from. |
| **Customer** | A person who buys something from the store. |
| **Invoice** | Another word for a receipt — a record of what was bought and for how much. |
| **Pack / Box** | A product that can be sold either as a full box or as single loose units, like a case of drinks. |
| **Refund** | Giving a customer their money back for some or all of what they bought. |
| **Void** | Fully cancelling a sale that was rung up by mistake, rather than a genuine customer return. |
| **On Credit** | When a trusted customer takes items now and pays for them later. |
